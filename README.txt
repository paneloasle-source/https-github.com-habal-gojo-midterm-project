SAFE & EQUAL 

Central issue:
Gender-based violence and sexual harassment in the Philippines.

How to use:
1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Replace the four [Name] placeholders in the footer with your group members.
3. If required by your instructor, add your school/course/section details.
4. The site is responsive for desktop and mobile.
5. Internet is only needed for the external reference links.

Included features:
- Responsive one-page design
- Central gender-related issue
- Philippine statistics with source attribution
- Philippine laws: RA 11313, RA 9262, RA 7877
- Interactive scenario/knowledge check
- Awareness, support, prevention and action sections
- References/further reading
