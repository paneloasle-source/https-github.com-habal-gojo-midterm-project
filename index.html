01 • Central issue

Gender-based violence is more than a “private problem.”

Gender-based violence can cause physical, sexual, psychological, emotional, or economic harm. Harassment can also happen in public spaces, schools, workplaces, and online environments.

🛡️

Recognize

Notice unwanted sexual remarks, threats, stalking, coercion, non-consensual sharing of images, and other behavior that violates safety, dignity, or consent.

🤝

Support

Listen without blaming. Respect the person's choices and privacy. When immediate danger exists, prioritize safety and contact appropriate authorities or support services.

📣

Prevent

Challenge victim-blaming, sexist jokes, harassment, and stereotypes. Build spaces where consent, dignity, accountability, and inclusion are normal.

03 • Know your rights

Philippine laws that address harassment and violence

These laws illustrate that gender equality and personal safety are supported by legal protections—not merely social expectations.

RA 11313 • 2019

Safe Spaces Act

Defines and penalizes gender-based sexual harassment in streets, public spaces, online, workplaces, and educational or training institutions.

Read the law ↗
RA 9262 • 2004

Anti-VAWC Act

Addresses violence against women and their children, including physical, sexual, psychological, and economic abuse in covered intimate relationships.

Read the law ↗
RA 7877 • 1995

Anti-Sexual Harassment Act

Declares sexual harassment unlawful in employment, education, and training environments and establishes duties for institutions and responsible persons.
